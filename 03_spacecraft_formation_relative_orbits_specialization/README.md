# Spacecraft Formation Relative Orbits Specialization

This folder will contain my personal notes, derivations, simulations, and engineering exercises developed while studying the University of Colorado Boulder **[Spacecraft Formation Relative Orbits Specialization](https://www.coursera.org/specializations/spacecraft-formation-relative-orbits)** on Coursera.

The specialization extends classical single-spacecraft orbital mechanics toward the modelling, analysis, design, and control of **relative spacecraft motion and formations**.

Topics covered throughout the specialization include:

* relative-motion coordinate descriptions;
* nonlinear and linearized relative equations of motion;
* bounded relative-orbit geometries;
* relative motion about circular, elliptical, and other reference orbits;
* orbital-element differences and curvilinear relative coordinates;
* effects of orbital perturbations such as (J_2);
* formation design that exploits orbital dynamics and perturbations;
* relative-motion stability and feedback control;
* rendezvous, proximity operations, inspection, servicing, and formation-flying applications.

The specialization concludes with a capstone project applying these concepts to a spacecraft servicing and relative-motion problem.

## Purpose

The material in this folder is intended to reinforce the specialization through my own:

* mathematical derivations and explanations;
* numerical simulations;
* relative-orbit visualizations;
* control-law implementations;
* engineering analysis and experimentation.

The objective is to develop both an intuitive and mathematical understanding of spacecraft relative motion before applying these concepts to more advanced rendezvous, proximity operations, formation-flying, and autonomous spacecraft problems.
