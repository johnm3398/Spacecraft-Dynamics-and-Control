# Advanced Spacecraft Dynamics and Control Specialization

This folder contains my personal notes, derivations, simulations, and engineering exercises developed while studying the University of Colorado Boulder **[Advanced Spacecraft Dynamics and Control Specialization](https://www.coursera.org/specializations/advanced-spacecraft-dynamics-control)** on Coursera.

This specialization builds on the spacecraft kinematics, rigid-body dynamics, and nonlinear control foundations developed in the preceding Spacecraft Dynamics and Control Specialization.

The advanced material extends these concepts toward more complex spacecraft systems, including:

* spacecraft with momentum-exchange devices;
* reaction wheels and control moment gyroscopes;
* momentum management and device singularities;
* analytical mechanics for complex spacecraft systems;
* constrained dynamical systems;
* spacecraft with flexible or time-varying components;
* development and validation of higher-fidelity spacecraft dynamics simulations.

The work in this directory is intended to reinforce these topics through my own mathematical derivations and numerical implementations.
