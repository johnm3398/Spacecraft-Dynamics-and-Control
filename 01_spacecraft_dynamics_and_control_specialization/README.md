# Spacecraft Dynamics and Control Specialization

This folder contains my personal notes, derivations, numerical implementations, and engineering exercises developed while completing the University of Colorado Boulder **[Spacecraft Dynamics and Control Specialization](https://www.coursera.org/specializations/spacecraft-dynamics-control)** on Coursera.

The specialization provides the theoretical foundation for much of the spacecraft attitude dynamics and control work in this repository. It covers three main areas:

1. **Kinematics** — describing spacecraft orientation and rotational motion.
2. **Kinetics** — developing rigid-body equations of motion and studying rotational dynamics.
3. **Control** — designing and analysing nonlinear spacecraft attitude control laws.

The specialization concludes with a **Mars Mission Capstone**, where the kinematics, dynamics, guidance, and control concepts are combined in a numerical spacecraft simulation.
